Hi, I'm Zahraa!

I grew up watching my mum pottering around the kitchen, and that's where my love of baking began. After years working as a doctor, I've made a little pivot to follow that passion. I bake with fresh, local ingredients and draw on flavours from my travels.

Every bake is made with care, curiosity and a lot of love. I hope you enjoy eating them as much as I enjoy making them.
