# Homepage (index.html)

**Scope:** a single static homepage. **Mode:** Persuade, with a practical backbone (contact details, hours).

- **Audience and job:** locals, custom-cake clients and gift buyers, mostly arriving by phone from Instagram. They need to see what Dahlia bakes, whether the Shed is open today, and how to reach her.
- **Action:** get in touch. Instagram DM and email are primary, phone is secondary. Each bake, and the cake of the week, has a pre-filled "Ask about" email link. There are no forms and no ordering. The custom-cakes section was removed at the user's request on 2026-09-29.
- **Proof:** the bakes and Dahlia herself. There are no photos, menu, prices, hours, address or story yet, so these ship as marked placeholders. Sample menu entries are labelled as samples, and placeholder facts carry a wavy proof-mark underline.
- **Section order:** Cover, then Cake of the week (a sage band with one large rose seed packet labelled "Packed for the week of …", filled in by JS), then Catalogue (the menu, with a category filter), Meet Dahlia, Instagram, and a Back cover holding Find us, hours, contact and the footer.
- **Memorable moment:** the cover dahlia opens ring by ring, and the cover states whether the Shed is open right now.
- **Confirmed facts:** open every Friday at 131 Dee Rd, Reading RG30 4JQ. The address is shown large on the cover card and at display size in Find the Shed, and JS states the next Friday. Dahlia's photo fills Meet Dahlia as a chocolate duotone on a pink plate with a pinked seal.
- **Unresolved:** phone, email, Friday opening times, delivery area, the Instagram handle (unverified), product photos, menu, prices and Dahlia's story.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Dahlia's Bakehouse as a grower's seed catalogue. Every bake is a numbered variety on a seed-packet card, every year a new edition, and the numbers are the references people quote when they message. It refuses the category default of a cream page with a photo hero and same-size rounded cards.

OWN-WORLD: spot-colour print. The only inks are cream paper, dahlia pink, sage, chocolate and one deep dahlia rose, laid as flat full-bleed bands with no gradients. Seed-packet cards have a colour field over a paper label, a dashed glue fold, and an authored two-tone dahlia print outlined in chocolate, with the dahlia form varying by category (decorative, cactus, pompon, waterlily). Young Serif for display, Schibsted Grotesk for text, pill buttons. This season's specials carry the deepest ink.

STORY: the visitor learns that this is one baker's small-batch catalogue, believes it's handmade and special, sees today's hours, and messages Dahlia quoting a bake number or a custom-cake brief.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a full-bleed pink cover under a pink running-head bar that holds the wordmark, the links and a Get in touch button. On the left, "Dahlia's / Bakehouse" at 6rem over the catalogue edition line, the tagline, Get in touch as the primary button and Explore our menu as the secondary, then a today's-hours card. On the right, a large dahlia print bleeding off the edge and opening on load.

FORM: seed catalogue, candidate 4 of my ordered list of seven (1 tiered cake, 2 cake box and ribbon, 3 recipe-card tin, 4 seed catalogue, 5 shed door and chalkboard, 6 letterpress invitation, 7 kraft market bag); seed key 20e7d342. Signature interaction: the bloom opens on load. Packets lift and their flower turns on hover. The filter rearranges the packets in place using view transitions and announces the change. Motion grammar: exponential ease-out from a visible starting state, disabled under reduced motion.
